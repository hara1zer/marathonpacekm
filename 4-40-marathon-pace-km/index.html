4:40 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 4:40 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 6:38.2/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 2:20:00 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How should I use the scenario on this page?

It shows the arithmetic consequence of a specific planning choice. Check the required effort against your current training and conditions before choosing to preserve the target.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.

What changes if you adjust the goal by five minutes?

A 4:45:00 goal averages 6:45.3/km, about 7.1 seconds/km slower than this plan. The calculator can show the revised checkpoints before you commit to a pace band. Five minutes may sound small as a finish-time choice, but keeping that difference for more than forty kilometres still requires evidence from your training.

Include a stop without confusing it with walking

If you stand still for one minute in total, keeping 4:40:00 would require covering the distance in 4:39:00, averaging 6:36.7/km while moving. This example assumes no distance is covered during the stop. Walking is different because it still contributes distance.

Make a plan using a pace band for this exact target. For a controlled start or negative split, the tool recalculates the later pace needed to preserve the finish time. Neither option guarantees that the required effort is sustainable.

Include a toilet stop in the elapsed plan

A three-minute stationary stop leaves less time to cover the measured distance. Recalculate before deciding whether to keep 4:40 or adopt a later finish. There is no need to recover the entire loss immediately.

4:40:00 − 180 seconds stationary = 4:37:00 to cover 42.195 km, or 6:33.9/km while moving. The reference including the pause is 6:38.2/km. This example assumes zero distance during the pause.
