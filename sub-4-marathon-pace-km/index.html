Sub-4 Marathon Pace Chart (3:59:59, 5:41/km)

To run a sub-4 marathon, you need to average about 5:41 per km or 9:09 per mile for 3:59:59. This page gives you the key split times, a full 1km cumulative pace chart, a printable pace band, and practical guidance on how to actually break 4 hours without letting the goal itself make you race too early. If you want the less psychological version of the same pace, compare this with 4:00 marathon pace or the slightly more aggressive 3:55 marathon pace.

Illustrative calculations checked 8 October 2026 · Distance, rounding and assumptions

Sub-four means elapsed time less than 4:00:00. This page’s editable preset is 3:59:59; it identifies the boundary, with only one second to spare. A rounded halfway display of 2:00:00 comes from an exact 1:59:59.5, not a time allowance.

A slow first 10K is not free time

If the first 10 km average 5:45/km, you reach the marker at 57:30. Finishing in 3:59:00 then leaves 3:01:30 over 32.195 km: 5:38.3/km for the rest, before further stops. Starting gently can be deliberate, but the later requirement must be visible.

Compare elapsed time at measured markers, including station time. If conditions or sustainable effort have changed, reconsider the finish goal rather than assuming the lost time can be earned back. Review weather and course adjustments and planning the first 10K.

3:59:59 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 3:59:59 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 5:41.2/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 2:00:00 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.
