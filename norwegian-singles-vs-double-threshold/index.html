Norwegian Singles vs double threshold: compare the whole day

A workload and logistics comparison, with a concrete example of how a second session changes more than the threshold label.

Published by Davin Pinto · Reviewed 1 October 2026 · Editorial policy · Corrections

A double puts two quality sessions on one day

In the elite context described by the linked sources, double threshold involves morning and afternoon threshold work inside a wider training system. The session arrangement is only one part of that context. Recovery between sessions, overall volume and intensity monitoring affect what the day means.

A single leaves one quality session on that day

Our use of singles refers to one controlled threshold session on a quality day. It does not define how many quality days occur in the week. The overview separates this adaptation from a documented elite protocol.

Compare the dose and the time commitment

Hypothetical identical sessions to isolate the effect of a double
MeasureOne sessionTwo sessions
Work: 4 × 6 min each24 min48 min
Recovery: 3 × 1 min each3 min6 min
Warm-up + cooldown: 10 + 10 min each20 min40 min
Running-session time47 min94 min
Getting changed, travel and between-session recoveryOne setupTwo setups plus a gap

This example doubles the work and running time. Real sessions vary; it is not an elite template or an instruction to do both.

Decide what problem a second session would solve

Write down the intended benefit and what would be removed elsewhere in the week. If the answer is only that a double resembles an elite routine, there is no individual workload case yet. A single quality day is easier to schedule, but it still needs to fit your current recovery.

Monitoring does not create capacity

A lactate meter or heart-rate strap can supply measurements; buying one does not supply training history or between-session recovery. A fixed number borrowed from another runner is not a personal threshold. Avoid using a reading to justify work that your wider week is not tolerating.

The long run belongs in the comparison

For a marathon block, count long-run pace work, hills and racing alongside threshold sessions. The second session may consume recovery that you wanted for a race-specific long run. The marathon adaptation example shows the scheduling tradeoff.

What would need to be assessed

A considered assessment would include sustained training history, current load, recovery opportunities, injury history and how intensity is monitored. That is an individual training decision. This article does not supply a mileage cutoff that qualifies someone for doubles.

Make the decision reviewable

Compare planned work minutes, total session time, the gap between sessions and the response of the next two easy days. Review what gets displaced. A larger day is not automatically a better day for your goal.

Comparison FAQ

Is a double just the same workout twice?

Not necessarily. The identical-session example isolates the workload difference. Published elite practice includes varying sessions within a broader system.

Does singles mean only one quality day each week?

No. It refers to one threshold session on a quality day in this site’s terminology.
