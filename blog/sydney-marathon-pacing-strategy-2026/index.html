Keep the edition visible

2026 archive: this page concerns the race on 30 August 2026. The organiser’s marathon page now promotes a later edition while retaining some 2026 course material. Do not assume that future dates, logistics or stations apply to this archive.

Use landmarks as effort cues

The course’s major cues include the North Sydney start, Harbour Bridge, Centennial Park, Mrs Macquarie’s Chair and the Opera House finish. Use the edition-labelled organiser map to place exact turns. This guide does not assign unverified kilometre coordinates to those landmarks.

The practical sequence is to settle through the start and bridge, assess effort in the middle sections and keep enough attention for the exposed late harbour approach.

Do not turn the bridge into a pace test

A climb changes the pace required for the same effort. If you lose seconds there, those seconds do not need to be repaid on the next descent. Treat the bridge as an effort cue, then check a measured marker once rhythm is settled. The hills guide develops that choice.

Use a measured 30 km checkpoint

For a four-hour reference, 30 km is 2:50:38. If you arrive in 2:55:00, the remaining 12.195 km have 1:05:00 available, requiring 5:19.8/km with no further pauses. That is much faster than the four-hour average of 5:41.3/km. Decide whether the effort fits before treating the remaining requirement as an instruction.

What happened before my walking breaks

At my own Sydney race, the effort became difficult before 30 km and I first walked around 31–32 km. The selected recorded split chart shows pace easing from 5:32 at km 25 to 6:50 at km 33, while recorded average heart rate stayed between 160 and 164 bpm.

Those observations do not establish a universal heart-rate limit or prove one cause. They make a useful case for responding to changing effort before trying to rescue the original finish target through faster running.

Print checkpoints; keep fuel reminders separate

Use the generator for even or revised checkpoints. The printed band does not include gel reminders. Save the fueling calculator’s separate elapsed schedule and compare it with the correct edition’s station map.

Do not carry station assumptions into another edition

Before a future Sydney race, verify the actual station distances, water, drink mix and gel availability on the current organiser guide. A brand name does not establish how much carbohydrate is in the amount you drink. Carry the servings you have rehearsed rather than assuming every station offers the same products.

My 2026 fuel account describes difficulty continuing my intended gel schedule. It is a personal observation, not a product comparison.
