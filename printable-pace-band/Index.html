How to print and wear the wrist band

  1. Select Print 3 wrist bands.
  2. Use A4 landscape and choose 100% or Actual size. Turn off “Fit to page”.
  3. Confirm the printed scale line measures exactly 50 mm.
  4. Cut around the dashed border, wrap the band around your wrist, and tape the marked overlap.
  5. Cover the front with clear tape before the race if you want extra sweat and rain protection.

The print job contains only three pace bands and the scale guide—not the generator, FAQ, or webpage preview.

Marathon pace band FAQ

What checkpoints should be on a marathon pace band?

Readability matters more than squeezing in every split. The wrist band uses ten major checkpoints: every 5 km with halfway and the finish, or equivalent three-mile checkpoints when miles are selected. A complete kilometre- or mile-by-mile table remains available underneath the previews.

Should I use even pace, a controlled start, or a negative split?

Even pace is the simplest plan. Controlled start gives you a deliberate cushion at the 5K checkpoint to discourage an early surge, then makes a very small adjustment across the remaining distance. The negative-split option increases speed progressively, so there is no abrupt pace change at halfway.

Will the controlled start make me miss my goal time?

No. With the default setting, your controlled 5K time is 15 seconds slower than the even-plan 5K time. Those 15 seconds are spread across the remaining 37.2 km, requiring an adjustment of only about 0.4 seconds per kilometre.

How do I know the band printed at the right size?

Print at 100% or Actual size, then measure the supplied 50 mm line. If the line is not exactly 50 mm, disable “Fit to page” or other scaling in the print dialog and try again.

Can I share my pace plan?

Yes. Copy the share link to preserve the goal time, units, pacing strategy, and optional race details. The phone card can also be downloaded as a PNG.
