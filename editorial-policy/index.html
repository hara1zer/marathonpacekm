Editorial policy and corrections

Written 21 September 2026

Responsibility and scope

Davin Pinto maintains Marathon Pace KM. The site combines pace calculations, general running guidance and personal experience. A target time is not a fitness assessment, and a generated plan is a starting point to adapt rather than individual coaching.

Three kinds of evidence

  • Calculations: distances, formulas and rounding rules are described in the methods page. Worked examples should be reproducible.
  • Research: links should support the claim beside them. An association in a study does not prove why one runner slowed down or prescribe that person's ideal training load.
  • Personal experience: race and training observations are identified as such. Recorded figures and personal recollections are clearly attributed. Interpretations are kept separate from observations. Hypothetical examples are not presented as recorded race splits.

Writing and updates

Software, including AI tools, may assist with drafting, calculations and code checks. That assistance does not create running experience, professional qualifications or evidence. Named first-person stories must come from the runner's supplied records or account.

Update dates indicate actual changes to the page, not a promise that every linked source or event detail has been freshly checked. Event-year guides describe that edition; verify current arrangements with the organiser. The repository records changes to the site.

Commercial links

As of 8 October 2026, the site has no active Amazon Associates account or commission-earning product links. Some articles include product and book examples for context; their inclusion is not a commercial endorsement or a claim of hands-on testing. The site retains AdSense verification metadata, but does not currently load advertising scripts or show AdSense ads. Any future affiliate or advertising arrangement will be disclosed clearly, with appropriate consent controls before activation. First-hand testing is stated separately and supported by an actual account of use.

Corrections

Use Contact and corrections to report an incorrect calculation, misleading statement or broken feature. Include the page and enough detail to reproduce the problem. Corrections should preserve the distinction between facts and interpretation and update any affected examples or structured data.

Recent corrections

23 October 2026: corrected distance and exact-time handoffs from the pace calculator and conditions tool. Half-marathon exports now retain the selected distance; unsupported short-race band exports are disabled.

Combined overlapping wind, hill and split-strategy guides, retaining redirects from their former addresses. Removed a contradictory causal summary from the after-30K review. The new hill and wind scenarios are labelled calculations, not race observations.

A code or calculation check is different from field testing. These changes do not claim a new race, venue visit, product test or print-and-wear trial.
