Editorial policy and corrections

Written 21 September 2026

Responsibility and scope

Davin Pinto maintains Marathon Pace KM. The site combines pace calculations, general running guidance and personal experience. A target time is not a fitness assessment, and a generated plan is a starting point to adapt rather than individual coaching.

Three kinds of evidence

  • Calculations: distances, formulas and rounding rules are described in the methods page. Worked examples should be reproducible.
  • Research: links should support the claim beside them. An association in a study does not prove why one runner slowed down or prescribe that person's ideal training load.
  • Personal experience: race and training observations are identified as such. Watch timing is distinguished from official results, and missing data is stated. Hypothetical examples are not presented as recorded race splits.

Writing and updates

Software, including AI tools, may assist with drafting, calculations and code checks. That assistance does not create running experience, professional qualifications or evidence. Named first-person stories must come from the runner's supplied records or account.

Update dates indicate actual changes to the page, not a promise that every linked source or event detail has been freshly checked. Event-year guides describe that edition; verify current arrangements with the organiser. The repository records changes to the site.

Commercial links

The site includes advertising code and some affiliate product links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. A product listing or affiliate link does not mean that I have personally tested that product. Personal testing must be stated separately and supported by an actual account of use.

Corrections

Use Contact and corrections to report an incorrect calculation, misleading statement or broken feature. Include the page and enough detail to reproduce the problem. Corrections should preserve the distinction between facts and interpretation and update any affected examples or structured data.
