What is the Norwegian Method in running? Read the evidence in context

A source guide that separates documented elite practice, first-person training history and the recreational adaptations often called singles.

The phrase refers to a system, not one workout

The sources linked here discuss high-volume low-intensity training alongside controlled threshold work and monitoring. “Norwegian” is not a guarantee that every schedule carrying the label follows the same system. Check who the runners were, the overall training and how intensity was regulated.

Read the threshold discussion within the whole week

The 2023 Casado paper considers lactate-guided intervals inside a wider high-volume approach. Borrowing the interval format while ignoring the surrounding easy work changes the context. That paper is not a validation of a three-day recreational plan.

A double is a day arrangement, not a qualification

Two threshold sessions on a day add scheduling and recovery demands. The worked comparison shows that two hypothetical 24-minute work sessions total 48 minutes, before warm-up and cooldown. A fixed weekly-distance cutoff would not tell us whether an individual can tolerate that.

Singles is the adaptation described on this site

We use singles for one threshold session on a quality day. Our sample plan uses one such session each week as a cautious illustration. That weekly frequency is not the definition or a tested Norwegian protocol. Other uses of the informal term can differ.

Borrow a review process before a workload

Write down the purpose of each session; distinguish easy work from demanding work; count work minutes and recoveries; inspect the response of the wider week. Those choices remain useful even if you never use the Norwegian label or measure lactate.

The 2024 Norwegian perspective paper reports session models and best-practice recommendations from world-class coaches. Use it to understand that context, not to assign yourself a matching workload.

The marathon still needs a race-duration plan

Threshold training does not remove the need for sustained long-run preparation, fueling practice and course-specific effort. The marathon adaptation asks what happens when a faster session competes with the long-run priority.

A useful evidence checklist

  • Who was observed or studied?
  • Was the source a trial, a review, a practice description or personal history?
  • Which part of the wider training system are you copying?
  • What demand will you remove if you add a new session?

Evidence FAQ

Does the research validate the singles schedules on this site?

No. The calendars are illustrations by Marathon Pace KM. The linked sources explain elite practice and broader concepts.

Is measuring lactate required to run controlled intervals?

The elite lactate-guided model uses measurements. Recreational controlled intervals can be discussed without claiming that a race-pace estimate measures lactate threshold.
