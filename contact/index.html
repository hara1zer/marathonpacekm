Contact and corrections

Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Marathon Pace KM is maintained by Davin Pinto. You can send a correction or feature request through the site's public GitHub issue tracker. A GitHub account is required to post.

Open a correction or feature request

For a calculation problem, include the page URL, the inputs you entered, the result shown and what you expected. For a print problem, include your browser and whether you selected wrist bands or the checkpoint sheet.

Reports on GitHub are public. Avoid including medical information, account credentials or any details you do not want published. For a privacy enquiry, request a private contact method without posting the personal information itself.

What happens to corrections?

Reports can be checked against the calculator methods and the relevant source. Changes are recorded in the repository; the editorial policy explains how calculations, research and personal experience are distinguished.
