Privacy

Updated 21 September 2026

Who operates this site

Marathon Pace KM is maintained by Davin Pinto. Use the contact and corrections page for enquiries about the site or this notice.

Calculator inputs and saved plans

The site's calculation code runs in your browser. The monthly training planner saves a draft using your browser's local storage, under the key mpkm_marathonplanner_v1. This can include the training and injury-status answers you enter. Resetting the planner or clearing this site's browser data removes that local draft. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see it.

The tools do not require a site account. This does not mean that visiting the site is anonymous: hosting, analytics and advertising services can process network and device information.

Shared links and downloads

Sharing a calculator or training-plan link can put your inputs in the URL, including training answers and any optional runner or race name. Anyone receiving that link can read those values. URLs can also appear in browser history and requests to the hosting service. Leave personal details out of a link you intend to share publicly.

CSV, calendar, phone-card and printed/PDF outputs are generated on your device. You control where you save or send those files.

Hosting and analytics

The site uses Cloudflare hosting and includes Cloudflare Web Analytics on some pages. Requests can expose information such as an IP address, requested URL, browser information and request time to the provider. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for its handling of personal information.

Google advertising

Some pages load Google AdSense advertising code. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies and information from visits to this and other websites to provide and measure advertising. Ad availability depends on the account and applicable consent settings.

Read how Google uses information from sites using its services and Google's advertising information. You can manage personalised advertising in My Ad Center and manage cookies through your browser. Where a consent message is shown, use its controls to manage the choices offered.

Affiliate links and other websites

Some product links are affiliate links. Marathon Pace KM may earn a commission when you follow one and make a qualifying purchase. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. The retailer processes your visit and purchase under its own privacy policy; this site does not receive your payment-card details through those links.

Correction requests and your choices

The contact page links to GitHub. Information you choose to post there is public and is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement. Do not post sensitive personal information. You can clear local site data, manage browser cookies, remove personal details before sharing a link and use the providers' privacy controls.

This notice describes the implemented features. Material changes to those features should be reflected here with a new update date.
