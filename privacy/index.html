Privacy

Updated 8 October 2026

Who operates this site

Marathon Pace KM is maintained by Davin Pinto. Use the contact and corrections page for enquiries about the site or this notice.

Calculator inputs and saved plans

The site's calculation code runs in your browser. The monthly training planner saves a draft using your browser's local storage, under the key mpkm_marathonplanner_v1. This can include the training and injury-status answers you enter. Resetting the planner or clearing this site's browser data removes that local draft. Other people using the same browser profile may be able to see it.

The tools do not require a site account. This does not mean that visiting the site is anonymous: hosting, analytics and advertising services can process network and device information.

Shared links and downloads

Sharing a calculator or training-plan link can put your inputs in the URL, including training answers and any optional runner or race name. Anyone receiving that link can read those values. URLs can also appear in browser history and requests to the hosting service. Leave personal details out of a link you intend to share publicly.

CSV, calendar, phone-card and printed/PDF outputs are generated on your device. You control where you save or send those files.

Hosting and optional analytics

The site uses Cloudflare hosting and may use Cloudflare Web Analytics. Google Analytics 4 is optional: its tag is not loaded until you select “Allow analytics” on the site. You can decline without losing access to the calculators, and reopen “Analytics privacy choices” in the footer to change your decision. Your choice is saved on this device in local storage; if storage is blocked, the site may ask again on your next visit.

The site sends GA4 page locations and referring-page addresses without query strings or fragments, and sends only predefined categories for tool actions. This avoids including runner names or other shared-plan inputs in these site-authored events. Google may also collect automatic events according to the operator’s Analytics account settings. Analytics providers may still process technical information such as device, network and cookie identifiers. Changing your choice to “Decline” disables the Google tag and reloads the page; no Google tag is loaded on subsequent visits while that choice is saved. Changing the choice in another tab also updates this tab. A consent update can be sent during withdrawal, and withdrawal cannot retract data sent previously. See Cloudflare's privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites using its services.

Google advertising

Google AdSense publisher identification remains in the page metadata and ads.txt for account verification, but this site does not currently load the AdSense advertising script or display AdSense ads. The analytics choice above is not advertising consent. Before advertising is activated, the operator must put appropriate regional consent controls in place and update this notice.

Google explains how information is used on partner sites. Your browser and provider settings may offer additional controls.

External links and commercial relationships

As of 8 October 2026, Marathon Pace KM has no active Amazon Associates account and does not use commission-earning shopping links. Product names shown in articles are informational examples, not retailer links or claims of personal testing. If we add affiliate links in future, they will be clearly disclosed here and near the relevant links. External websites handle their own visitors and purchases under their own privacy policies; Marathon Pace KM does not collect payment-card details.

Correction requests and your choices

The contact page links to GitHub. Information you choose to post there is public and is handled by GitHub under its privacy statement. Do not post sensitive personal information. You can clear local site data, manage browser cookies, remove personal details before sharing a link and use the providers' privacy controls.

This notice describes the implemented features. Material changes to those features should be reflected here with a new update date.
