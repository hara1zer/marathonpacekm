3:50 readiness checklist

A fast way to sanity-check whether 3:50 is realistic on the day.

  • Long run durability: you can run 28–32 km comfortably (ideally with the last 6–10 km steady).
  • Weekly consistency: you’ve stacked consistent weeks (volume matters more than one big session).
  • Fuel plan practiced: your gut tolerates carbs every ~30 minutes.
  • Pacing discipline: you can hold goal effort without frequent surges.

If you miss one box, keep the early pacing conservative and make the “push” decision after 30 km.

3:50 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 3:50 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 5:27.1/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 1:55:00 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How should I use the scenario on this page?

It shows the arithmetic consequence of a specific planning choice. Check the required effort against your current training and conditions before choosing to preserve the target.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.

What changes if you adjust the goal by five minutes?

A 3:55:00 goal averages 5:34.2/km, about 7.1 seconds/km slower than this plan. The calculator can show the revised checkpoints before you commit to a pace band. Five minutes may sound small as a finish-time choice, but keeping that difference for more than forty kilometres still requires evidence from your training.

Include a stop without confusing it with walking

If you stand still for one minute in total, keeping 3:50:00 would require covering the distance in 3:49:00, averaging 5:25.6/km while moving. This example assumes no distance is covered during the stop. Walking is different because it still contributes distance.
