Build a five-hour race plan

For a 5:00:00 finish, use 2:30:00 halfway as the even-pace reference and compare elapsed time at official course markers. The running pace from the run/walk calculator can be different from the overall race average.

Check the event’s cutoff rules, station locations and timing method before choosing the goal. If you plan to walk, practise the intervals early in long runs and measure your walking pace. If conditions require a slower target, update the goal above rather than treating the original finish time as mandatory.

A pace band is a reference, not a demand to catch up after every hill. The conditions tool and mid-race decision guide help review the plan.

Plan fuel for the full 5:00 duration

If your already-practised intake is 60 g/hour, the arithmetic total over 5:00 is 300 g. This is a worked example, not an intake recommendation based on finish speed. Count carbohydrates in your actual gels and drinks and compare them with the scheduled total.

Use the fueling calculator for a timed menu and the worked finish-time schedules for checked examples. A pace band supplies split times; your fuel reminders are a separate plan.

Is a five-hour target realistic?

A recent shorter race gives a starting estimate, but it does not establish how the last 12 kilometres will go. Use the marathon time predictor, then compare the result with training consistency, long-run execution and recovery.

A few useful answers

5:00 marathon pace FAQ

These reference answers use the default finish target. The calculator can show a different plan.

What pace per km is a 5:00 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 7:06.6/km, or approximately 7:07/km when rounded to a whole second.

What pace per mile is a 5:00 marathon?

The average is 11:26.5/mile, or approximately 11:27/mile rounded to a whole second. One mile is exactly 1.609344 km.

What is the halfway split for 5:00?

Even pacing reaches 21.0975 km at 2:30:00 elapsed, including any stops.

Can I print these splits or save a phone card?

The pace-band generator opens with the selected finish time. It supports kilometre or mile checkpoints, wrist bands, an A4 checkpoint sheet and a downloadable PNG phone card. PDF saving uses the browser print dialog.

Can I run/walk a five-hour marathon?

The calculator shows the running pace required for your chosen intervals, walking pace and stationary stop allowance. It accounts for a partial final interval. Whether that pace is sustainable depends on your preparation and conditions.

Does walking at aid stations count as stopped time?

Walking covers distance and belongs in the walking intervals. Stopped time means standing still. Counting the same station minutes in both places would overstate the time allowance.
