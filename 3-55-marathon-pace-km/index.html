Choose a sustainable margin below four hours

A 3:55 reference leaves five minutes below four hours. That allowance disappears if you use a four-hour moving pace plus pauses. All finish goals here include stops.

What the five-minute margin means

If you cover the distance in 3:55 moving time, two stationary minutes produce 3:57 elapsed; five produce 4:00, which is no longer sub-four. This arithmetic assumes no distance during the stops. A 3:55 elapsed plan already includes pauses, so do not add a stop allowance twice.

Choosing the faster moving pace still needs evidence from your preparation. The sub-four guide compares boundary targets; the predictor explains the uncertainty in converting a shorter race.

3:55 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 3:55 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 5:34.2/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 1:57:30 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.
