3:00 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 3:00 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 4:16.0/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 1:30:00 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How should I use the scenario on this page?

It shows the arithmetic consequence of a specific planning choice. Check the required effort against your current training and conditions before choosing to preserve the target.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.

Account for the full 3:00 duration

If you have rehearsed 60 g/h, the arithmetic total for 3:00 is 180.0 g. That chosen intake is an example, not a recommendation based on your speed. Count actual product grams and planned drinks; a spare carried but not consumed does not contribute.

Use the fueling calculator for timestamps and supplied average, and the complete worked menus for a checked example. Nutrition research supplies context; duration supplies the multiplication.

4:16/km is a rounded label, not a sub-three guarantee

Exactly 4:16/km over 42.195 km takes about 3:00:02. That is just outside three hours. The exact three-hour average is a little faster, at 4:15.95/km. A watch displaying whole seconds cannot tell you which side of the line you are on.

If your aim is strictly under three hours, choose an explicit finish time below 3:00:00 in the calculator and carry its checkpoints. A 2:59:00 target, for example, averages about 4:14.5/km. That is a mathematical example, not evidence that your fitness supports the extra minute. Any qualifying standard and timing rules must be checked with the relevant organiser.

Rehearse the decisions, not just the speed

At the 10 km checkpoint you have more than 32 km left. A quick early split cannot establish that the goal was conservative. Use your preparation to decide how much effort is sustainable on climbs and where you can take fuel without surging afterwards.

A fast half marathon or a completed interval session gives different evidence from a controlled long run. Check whether marathon-pace work fits inside ordinary training and whether you recover well enough to continue the week. The goal page cannot prescribe a universal mileage requirement or turn a single session into a pass/fail test.

Include a stop without confusing it with walking

If you stand still for one minute in total, keeping 3:00:00 would require covering the distance in 2:59:00, averaging 4:14.5/km while moving. This example assumes no distance is covered during the stop. Walking is different because it still contributes distance.
