Plan access through the Box Hill facility information

Hagenauer Reserve’s athletics track is at Elgar Road and Willow Street in Box Hill North. Box Hill Athletic Club publishes facility and hire information. A 2023 council comparison records public use of the track; current club and hire arrangements determine how to plan a particular session.

For runners in Melbourne’s east, this is a measured option close to Box Hill. The practical reason to choose it is an available track period that fits the workout. Use the club’s current information when setting a fixed arrival time or organising several runners.

Surface, lights and reserve rules

The published specifications describe a 400 m polyurethane circuit with eight circular lanes; the independent directory lists ten lanes on the straight. Floodlighting is documented by the club and council. That establishes installed lighting rather than a promise about its operation during a casual evening visit.

The council’s reserve rules prohibit dogs on the track and infield. Plan a run with a dog elsewhere, even if the surrounding park has different arrangements. A group using the athletics facility should organise its hire through Box Hill Athletic Club.

A measured workout in the east

A 1.2 km repetition is three lane-one laps. At 4:30/km it takes 5:24, with each 400 m checkpoint at 1:48. That distance can fit a repetition workout already in your programme without constructing a road loop around Elgar Road.

If the available period is limited by another allocation, allow for the whole workout rather than just the faster repetitions. Follow the venue’s current lane arrangements; a full circuit outside lane one should not be treated as the same 400 m distance.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Getting there

The club lists car and bus parking. The transport directory identifies Hagenauer Reserve/Elgar Road as a nearby bus stop. Use current journey planning for the service and walking connection rather than relying on an old timetable or assuming a particular route frequency.

Use the Elgar Road/Willow Street location and the club’s facility information together when choosing an entrance. For a comparison within the eastern suburbs, Tom Kelly Athletics Track has a published availability calendar; Knox Park publishes turnstile hours. Those access details are more useful to a fixed-time workout than a general list of track features.

Frequently asked questions

Is this the Box Hill athletics track?

Yes. Hagenauer Reserve's athletics facility is at Elgar Road and Willow Street in Box Hill North; Box Hill Athletic Club publishes its facility information.

What public-access evidence is available?

A 2023 council comparison records public use. Consult current club and hire arrangements for the session you intend to run.

Are dogs allowed on the track?

Council rules prohibit dogs on the athletics track and infield.

Is floodlighting documented?

The club and council document floodlighting. This guide does not infer a casual-use lighting timetable from that feature.
