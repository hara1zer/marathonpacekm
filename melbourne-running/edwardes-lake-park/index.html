Edwardes Lake Park Running Track, Reservoir

A northern 400 m venue with council-documented free access. The dated damage and incident record also matters when planning a visit.

Access evidence and current conditions

Edwardes Lake Park Athletics Track is in Reservoir, around Griffiths Street and Edwardes Street. Darebin’s March 2026 council minutes describe free public access throughout the year, including 24-hour access. That is a dated council record, rather than a guarantee about an open gate on any particular visit.

The same minutes record damage associated with e-bikes and antisocial incidents. This matters when choosing the venue for a faster session: inspect the running line before relying on it for repeated efforts. The access record does not establish lighting or personal safety after dark.

What the track offers

The specialist directory lists a 400 m synthetic circuit, six circular lanes and eight lanes on the straight, with the surface reported as Spurtan BV. The council’s park listing confirms public toilets and a drinking fountain in Edwardes Lake Park. Plan the workout around the track and the park facilities, following their current signs.

For a Reservoir runner seeking a measured session, the combination of a northern location and documented free access is the main reason to consider it. For a session where darkness or surface condition would be a problem, choose the arrival time accordingly rather than using the 24-hour record as reassurance.

Using the 400 m circuit

A 1.6 km repetition is four lane-one laps. At 5:00/km it takes 8:00, with 2:00 per lap. That gives a simple checkpoint for a session already in your plan while keeping the repetition length tied to the marked circuit.

Keep the session flexible if the inspection shows a damaged or obstructed section. Moving outward changes a full-lap distance; repeatedly weaving around an obstruction also changes the work. A different northern venue may serve the intended measured workout more clearly that day.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Practical visit plan

Use the council’s Edwardes Lake Park listing for the location and amenities. Set your arrival point around Griffiths Street or Edwardes Street, then locate the athletics track and public facilities before beginning. Bring sufficient water if the session depends on short recoveries rather than a trip to the park fountain.

For a very early or late workout, consider the conditions on arrival and the dated incident record alongside the access policy. The Sprint Athletics Centre guide offers another northern option with an explicit dawn-to-dusk policy and booking exclusions.

Frequently asked questions

Is Edwardes Lake Park track free?

Darebin's March 2026 council minutes describe free public access year-round. Current signs and conditions remain relevant to the visit.

Does 24-hour access mean the track is lit all night?

The council access record does not establish a lighting timetable. This guide makes no all-night lighting claim.

What surface and lanes are listed?

The directory reports a synthetic Spurtan BV surface, six circular lanes and eight straight lanes, on a 400 m circuit.

Why check the surface before faster work?

The March 2026 council record notes e-bike damage and antisocial incidents. Inspect the current running line rather than assuming the access policy establishes its condition.
