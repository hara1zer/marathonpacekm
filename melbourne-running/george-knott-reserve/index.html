What to plan around at George Knott

The six-lane circuit at George Knott Reserve gives Clifton Hill runners an inner-north option for a measured workout. It is on Heidelberg Road in the City of Yarra. The published specifications list a 400 m synthetic circuit, with eight lanes on the straight.

The important planning question is whether the track is available when you want it. The community facility guide reports public use outside club activities and events. Group hire goes through Collingwood Harriers. Use the council and club information before arranging a coached session or travelling with a group; community access does not override an existing booking.

Access and reserve facilities

Yarra lists public toilets, a drinking fountain and parking at the reserve. Those facilities make it practical to organise a workout around this location, but allow time to find the current entrances and signs before starting. Use the Heidelberg Road reserve address as your journey destination.

Dogs are prohibited inside the fenced athletics facility. Yarra also requires a permit for personal trainers using its public spaces. A runner doing an individual session and a trainer bringing clients have different access considerations: organise the permission and hire arrangements for the actual activity.

A session that fits this circuit

If your programme includes 2 km repetitions, each is five lane-one laps. On a six-lane circuit, choose a quiet available period and follow the lane arrangements used by the people already training. Keep a longer block adaptable: a club arriving or an event starting can matter more than the distance you had written down.

At 5:00/km, a 2 km block takes 10 minutes. Use the table below for the 400 m checkpoints within that block. If you need to move into an outer lane, the full circuit is longer; retain the actual marked repetition distance when interpreting the time.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Before travelling

Start with the council’s reserve listing for location, facilities and restrictions, then the community access guide for the public-use arrangement. This page uses that published arrangement without assigning a daily opening timetable. If your workout depends on a specific time, establish that the club or event allocation leaves room for it.

For an organised group, contact the hire operator before settling the session time. For an individual workout, have another time or venue available if the track is occupied. The Melbourne directory lets you compare other inner and northern options by their own access notes.

Frequently asked questions

Can the public run at George Knott Reserve?

The community facility guide reports public use outside club activities and events. Organised hire goes through Collingwood Harriers; current allocations take priority.

How many lanes does George Knott have?

The published track specifications list six circular lanes and eight on the straight, with a 400 m lane-one circuit.

Can I bring my dog onto the track?

Yarra prohibits dogs inside the fenced athletics facility. Follow the reserve's current signs.

Are water and toilets documented?

Yarra lists a drinking fountain and public toilets at the reserve. Locate the current facilities before starting the session.
