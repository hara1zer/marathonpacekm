Lakeside is a booked training session

Lakeside Stadium at 33 Aughtie Drive, Albert Park, suits runners who can arrange a workout around a public booking slot. State Sport Centres publishes public training on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 4–8 pm, and Saturday from 9 am–noon. Events and availability in the booking portal take priority over that timetable.

Book through the operator before travelling. The facility is cashless. Treat the price and availability shown for your booking as the operative information, rather than interpreting a nearby park’s public access as stadium entry.

Why use Lakeside for a marathon workout?

The 400 m synthetic track provides a measured alternative to the nearby Albert Park lake paths. A longer interval session can fit the published afternoon window, subject to your booking. For example, a 3 km effort is seven and a half measured laps; two such efforts give 6 km of faster work without turning the lake’s approximate circuit into an interval distance.

At 4:30/km, 3 km takes 13:30. Work backwards from the booked session’s finish time and include your warm-up and recoveries. The usable booking window, rather than the stadium’s presence or installed lights, determines when that workout can happen.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Getting to the stadium

The operator identifies tram 96, stop 129, and tram 12, stop 131, as access options. Plan the current service and walking connection to the stadium entrance, particularly if travelling for the Saturday slot. The street address is within Port Phillip’s Albert Park precinct.

State Sport Centres lists 418 parking bays managed by Parks Victoria. Check the park’s parking signs and payment arrangements; the track booking and car parking are separate parts of the visit. Arrival, parking and entry checks all consume time before the workout.

Facilities and an alternative run

The official Albert Park map marks public toilets and drinking fountains in the surrounding park. Use it to plan facilities near your arrival point without assuming every park amenity is inside the stadium. Bring what you need for the booked session.

If a stadium event removes the public slot, the Albert Park Lake guide provides a nearby path-based alternative. Its approximately 4.7 km circuit serves a different purpose: longer aerobic laps rather than an exact replacement for a measured track interval. Make that change to the session deliberately.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a booking to run at Lakeside?

The operator requires a booking for public training. Use the portal for availability and the price of the selected session.

When are public training sessions listed?

The published slots are Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 4–8 pm and Saturday 9 am–noon. Events and portal availability take priority.

Which trams serve Lakeside?

The operator lists tram 96 stop 129 and tram 12 stop 131. Plan the current service and walking connection before travelling.

Is a lake lap the same distance as a stadium lap?

No. The stadium circuit is 400 m; Parks Victoria describes the separate lake circuit as approximately 4.7 km.
