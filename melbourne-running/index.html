Running Tracks and Paths in Melbourne

Choose a measured track for repetitions or a path for time on feet. Compare the access rules and training trade-offs before you travel.

  • Metric distances

Start with the session you need

Choose around the venue’s actual arrangements. Lakeside uses booked public sessions; Sprint publishes dawn-to-dusk entry; Newport directs casual runners to middle and outer lanes. For a path run, compare Albert Park’s approximate lake lap with The Tan’s official 3.827 km circuit and Anderson Street climb.

These tracks give coverage across inner, northern, eastern, south-eastern and western Melbourne. Published public-access information informed the selection; the notes retain booking exclusions and dates where the evidence is older. The paths have documented running events, club use or recommendations in official and local running guides. This is a training shortlist rather than a numerical popularity ranking.

Clifton Hill · Yarra · inner north

George Knott Reserve

400 m · 6 circular lanes · synthetic

A measured option for inner-north intervals. Yarra allows community use outside club activities and events; Collingwood Harriers handles track hire. The council lists toilets and water, and prohibits dogs inside the track fence.

Albert Park · Port Phillip · inner south

Lakeside Stadium

400 m · synthetic · booked public sessions

Choose it when you can plan a track session around a published booking slot. State Sport Centres lists public sessions on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday afternoons and Saturday mornings, subject to events and booking availability. Check its current portal before travelling.

Craigieburn · Hume · north

Sprint Athletics Centre

400 m · 8 circular lanes · synthetic

Hume publishes free dawn-to-dusk entry outside exclusive bookings. A useful northern choice for counting interval laps and controlling recovery distance. Public toilets are in adjacent Craigieburn Anzac Park.

Reservoir · Darebin · north

Edwardes Lake Park Track

400 m · 6 circular lanes · synthetic

Darebin's March 2026 council minutes describe free, round-the-clock access. That makes it a northern option for flexible session timing. The same record notes e-bike damage and antisocial behaviour; inspect the surface and current conditions before starting fast repetitions.

Greensborough · Banyule · north-east

Willinda Park

400 m · 8 circular lanes · polyurethane

Adds a measured option in the north-east. A 2023 council comparison records public use outside bookings; use the current operator's contact details to plan a session. Its published daytime hire hours describe bookings, rather than unrestricted public running hours.

Aberfeldie · Moonee Valley · north-west

Moonee Valley Athletics Centre

400 m · 8 circular lanes · synthetic

An option for north-western runners who want marked repetitions. The community facility guide reports public access around exclusive club and school hire. Moonee Valley manages reserve bookings, so organised group sessions need a separate booking check.

Newport · Hobsons Bay · west

Newport Park Athletics Track

400 m lane-one circuit · 8 circular lanes · synthetic

The council permits public training but directs casual runners to the middle and outer lanes. Those laps are longer than 400 m, so use the correct marked start for a measured repetition. The 2026/27 rules allow 5 mm or 7 mm spikes and prohibit animals, bikes and ball games.

Truganina · Wyndham · west

Fortitude Drive Reserve

Synthetic athletics facility · public access around tenant use

Included to cover the growing western suburbs. Wyndham allows public use around tenant activities. Tenant use can restrict access, especially in the evenings and on weekends. The pavilion is a later project stage; plan facilities and any group hire through the council's current updates.

400 m · 8 circular lanes · polyurethane

An eastern alternative for measured efforts. Box Hill Athletic Club publishes facility and hire information; the public-access evidence is a 2023 council comparison. The club prohibits dogs on the track and infield, and lists floodlights and car parking.

Doncaster East · Manningham · east

Tom Kelly Athletics Track

400 m · 8 circular lanes · synthetic

Useful for eastern-suburb intervals when the availability calendar shows public access. Club sessions have exclusive periods, so consult the published calendar rather than assuming an open gate means the track is available for your workout.

Murrumbeena · Glen Eira · south-east

Duncan Mackinnon Reserve Track

400 m · 8 circular lanes · synthetic

Glen Eira permits public running in daylight outside bookings. Its 6 am–10 pm facility hours do not grant unrestricted track access: floodlights operate for bookings, and summer club exclusions occupy several afternoons and weekend periods. Read the council's current timetable first.

Knox Park Athletics Centre

400 m · 8 circular lanes · Conispur SW synthetic

Knox publishes daily 5 am–9 pm turnstile access, with exclusions for bookings, maintenance and mowing. The council records a Conispur SW resurfacing in 2025. A south-eastern option for planned repetitions with a clear published access window.

6 established running paths

Royal Botanic Gardens / Kings Domain · inner Melbourne

The Tan

3.827 km official lap · gravel and sealed hill

Repeated loops suit controlled aerobic running and practising uphill effort. The published Saturday 8–10 am run club and the annual Run the Tan event provide evidence of established running use. Anderson Street changes the effort of every clockwise lap.

Read the training guide →

Sources: Event course distance · Council run-club listing.

CBD towards Westerfolds Park · inner and east

Main Yarra Trail

33 km in Parks Victoria's trail listing · lower asphalt sections

A longer corridor for building a run around a chosen turnaround. Visit Victoria includes it in Melbourne's running trails, while Sri Chinmoy stages running events around Burnley. The Gipps Street ramp opened in February 2026; check current works before committing to a continuous session.

Read the training guide →

Sources: Parks Victoria trail listing · Running-event evidence · Gipps Street ramp update.

Albert Park · Port Phillip · inner south

Albert Park Lake

Approximately 4.7 km lake circuit · sealed and unsealed paths

A flat loop option for repeating a steady effort and returning to the same starting area. Parks Victoria recommends the park for running and documents its Saturday 8 am parkrun. Use the lake circuit distance as approximate; the event's 5 km course is a separate route.

Sources: Parks Victoria running guide · Park and visitor information.

Yarra, Merri Creek, Inner Circle, Royal Park and Docklands

Capital City Trail

Approximately 29 km circuit · shared trail and street sections

A varied long-run circuit, with crossings that make uninterrupted tempo work harder to organise. Melbourne Water and Merri-bek describe the route, and local running guides include it. Council planning records heavy cyclist use; leave room to share the path.

Port Melbourne to Seaford · bayside corridor

Bay Trail

Choose a section and turnaround · sealed northern sections, gravel farther south

A coastal option for an out-and-back long run and practising effort into changing wind. Port Phillip's event notifications document organised running along the St Kilda and Elwood foreshore. Check the event and closure notices for your chosen section before a faster session.

Dights Falls to the Western Ring Road · inner north and north

Merri Creek Trail

21 km in Merri-bek's trail description · shared creek corridor

A northern option for an easy out-and-back or extending time on feet. Merri Creek Running Club's scheduled Monday 6 km sessions provide direct evidence of regular running use. Pick a turnaround for your session rather than treating the whole trail as a measured half-marathon course.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I run on a free public track in Melbourne?

Sprint Athletics Centre publishes free dawn-to-dusk entry outside exclusive bookings. Other venues have different club exclusions or public-session bookings; compare the access notes and follow the operator link before travelling.

Which Melbourne route suits a marathon long run?

Repeated Albert Park or Tan circuits keep you returning to the same starting area. Main Yarra, Merri Creek and Bay Trail sections allow out-and-back planning. Choose the surface, interruptions and distance that fit your existing session.

Are all athletics-track laps exactly 400 m?

The standard 400 m circuit describes lane one at its measurement line. Outer lanes are longer. Use the correct marked start for your chosen lane and repetition; do not apply lane-one lap times to an outer-lane full circuit.
