Tom Kelly Athletics Running Track, Doncaster East

A George Street track with a published availability calendar. Several weekday afternoons and weekend periods fall inside exclusive club use.

Check the availability calendar first

Tom Kelly Athletics Track is at 125A George Street, Doncaster East, in Manningham’s Rieschiecks Reserve. Public access is available outside exclusive bookings, and the local athletics centre publishes a track-availability calendar.

Doncaster Athletic Club lists exclusive use on Monday, Tuesday and Thursday from 4:30–7:30 pm, Sunday from 8 am–noon and most Saturday afternoons. Those allocations matter to a marathon runner fitting sessions around work: a weekday afternoon arrival can fall inside club use even when the wider reserve is accessible.

Use the availability calendar for the intended date rather than building a weekly plan around an empty-looking track. School or other bookings can add exclusions beyond the regular club periods.

What is documented at the reserve

The track directory describes a 400 m circuit with eight circular lanes and twelve straight lanes, with the synthetic surface reported as Rekortan M. Floodlighting is reported, but its presence does not override an exclusive allocation or establish a casual lighting schedule.

Earlier council documents identify toilets and water-fountain provision in the reserve. Bring the water needed for a continuous workout and locate current public amenities before starting. The age of those documents is retained here rather than treating their provision as a newly inspected service.

Fitting a longer block into an available period

A 5 km marathon-pace block is twelve and a half lane-one laps. At 5:00/km it takes 25 minutes. If the calendar leaves a suitable public window, that provides a measured option without navigating a street-based Doncaster route.

Finish the whole session within the available period, including warm-up and recovery time. If your usual slot falls inside a club allocation, use a different public time or compare another eastern venue rather than assuming you can train alongside an exclusive booking.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Arrival and alternatives

Use the council’s Rieschiecks Reserve listing for the George Street location and the athletics calendar for track availability. The two sources answer different practical questions: where the facility is and whether public running fits the date you have chosen.

Hagenauer Reserve in Box Hill North is another eastern measured-track option with its own club arrangements. The Main Yarra Trail is an alternative for a path-based long run, where junctions and turnaround planning matter more than a fixed circuit.

Frequently asked questions

When is public access available at Tom Kelly?

Public use is outside exclusive bookings. Use the local athletics centre's current availability calendar for the intended date.

What regular club periods are listed?

Doncaster Athletic Club lists Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 4:30–7:30 pm, Sunday 8 am–noon and most Saturday afternoons as exclusive periods.

Where is the track?

It is at 125A George Street, Doncaster East, in Manningham's Rieschiecks Reserve.

Does an open reserve mean the track is available?

No. Access to the wider reserve does not override an exclusive athletics-track booking.
