An Aberfeldie option around exclusive bookings

Moonee Valley Athletics Centre is in Aberfeldie Park. The community facility listing gives 20 Corio Street as the address and reports public use outside exclusive club and school bookings. Moonee Valley’s council booking page is the starting point for organised facility hire.

The key distinction is between an available public period and a booked session. A club or school allocation can take exclusive use, so establish the timing before arranging a group workout. The community access description is not a seven-day opening timetable.

Track details that affect the session

The directory lists a 400 m circuit, eight circular lanes and eight straight lanes, with the synthetic surface reported as Spurtan BV. Lighting is reported at the facility. Installed lights do not themselves provide a timetable for casual evening running.

For north-western runners, the useful feature is a measured venue in Aberfeldie rather than a journey across Melbourne for repetitions. Keep the travel and availability check part of the session plan, especially when a longer block needs a clear stretch of track time.

Planning a measured block

A 4 km marathon-pace block is ten lane-one laps. At 5:30/km that is 22 minutes, or 2:12 per lap. Use that calculation to assess whether the available period can accommodate the block plus the rest of your session.

If you are doing more than one block, account for recovery time before a school or club booking starts. The full outer-lane circuit is longer than 400 m; use the appropriate measured start if your session is moved to another lane.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Facilities and getting there

A government public-toilet listing covers the venue, and the community guide reports parking. Use the Corio Street address and current signs to organise arrival. A listing establishes that facilities are documented, rather than guaranteeing that a particular entrance or toilet is open when you arrive.

Keep the council’s booking information and community venue guide together when planning. The former explains how to request an organised allocation; the latter describes the track’s public-use arrangement. For another western option, Newport Park has a published casual lane-use rule that changes how to interpret a full-lap pace target.

Frequently asked questions

Can the public use the Aberfeldie athletics track?

The community guide describes public use outside exclusive club and school bookings. Use current booking information for a fixed-time session.

Where is the track listed?

The community listing gives 20 Corio Street, Aberfeldie, in the Moonee Valley area.

Is lighting documented?

The facility is reported as having lighting. This guide does not assign it a casual-use operating timetable.

How long is a 4 km track block?

It is ten measured lane-one laps. At 5:30/km, the block takes 22:00; a full outer-lane circuit is longer.
