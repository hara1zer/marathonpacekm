Duncan Mackinnon Reserve Running Track, Murrumbeena

Plan a daylight public session around the summer exclusion timetable. The reserve's separate 1.2 km perimeter path adds a warm-up option.

Daylight public access, not the full facility hours

Duncan Mackinnon Reserve’s athletics track is at North Road and Murrumbeena Road in Glen Eira. The council lists facility hours of 6 am–10 pm, but casual public track access is in daylight and outside bookings. Floodlights operate for booked use. The longer facility window should not be read as unrestricted evening running.

The published summer exclusions occupy several common training times. Private bookings can add further restrictions, so use the council page for the date you intend to visit.

The track and the separate perimeter path

The athletics facility has a 400 m all-weather synthetic circuit, eight circular lanes and ten straight lanes. Glen Eira also lists a 1.2 km perimeter path around the reserve. That second route can be useful for a warm-up or cool-down while keeping the faster, measured work on the athletics track when it is available.

One published perimeter circuit and two lane-one track laps add to 2 km arithmetically. Any connecting distance between them is extra. Keep the two routes separate in your session record rather than presenting the combination as a surveyed 2 km course.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Facilities and rules

The council locates public toilets on the car park side of the pavilion and documents parking in the reserve. Find that side of the building before the workout, particularly if using the perimeter path first. Bring enough water to avoid relying on a refill during a short recovery.

Dogs are prohibited on the athletics track. Booked groups exclude public use, and the presence of running activity inside the facility does not itself establish a public session.

When this venue makes sense

Duncan Mackinnon suits a Murrumbeena-area workout when a daylight public window and the separate perimeter route fit the plan. The exclusion timetable makes many summer afternoons and parts of the weekend poor default choices for casual track training.

For an alternative south-eastern venue, Knox Park publishes daily turnstile hours with event and maintenance exclusions. Compare actual access for the intended day rather than the headline facility closing times.

Frequently asked questions

Can casual runners use the track until 10 pm?

No. The 6 am–10 pm facility hours are separate from casual public track access, which is in daylight outside bookings.

Are lights available for casual training?

Glen Eira says track lighting operates for bookings only.

Is the perimeter path part of the 400 m circuit?

No. The council lists a separate 1.2 km reserve perimeter path; connecting distance between it and the athletics track is additional.

Where are the toilets listed?

The council locates public toilets on the car park side of the pavilion. Use current signs to find the public entrance.
