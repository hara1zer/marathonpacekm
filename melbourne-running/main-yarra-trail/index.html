Choose a section of the river corridor

The Main Yarra Trail gives Melbourne runners a corridor from the CBD riverfront towards Westerfolds Park. Parks Victoria lists a 33 km trail, while Visit Victoria includes the route in its Melbourne running guide. Use that published extent to understand the network rather than treating every entry point as the beginning of a 33 km measured course.

The lower shared route is described as asphalt. For a marathon session, choose the section, entry and turnaround together: a lower-river out-and-back and a longer journey towards Westerfolds are different runs. The published corridor distance does not establish the surface or facilities at every kilometre.

10 km, 21.1 km and 30 km options

An out-and-back lets you choose distance without claiming a new mapped circuit. The turn distances below are calculations from the session target, not named or surveyed points on the trail. Use the current route map and your recorded distance to select a continuous, open section with enough room for the intended outward leg.

The documented Burnley circuit

Sri Chinmoy’s recurring event provides a more specific running reference: a published 7 km Burnley circuit, with 7 km, 14 km and half-marathon event options. Consult the organiser’s current course information if using that circuit for training.

Two nominal 7 km laps give 14 km arithmetically. Do not reconstruct the half-marathon route as exactly three such laps; the organiser’s event course determines its measured distance. Event support, marshals and drink stations also belong to the organised event rather than an ordinary training visit.

The Gipps Street change

Parks Victoria confirms that the Gipps Street ramp opened in February 2026. That is useful when assessing older route descriptions that still refer to the former interruption. Use the current project information alongside any older running guide, and follow signs for subsequent works on your chosen section.

The ramp update does not establish that the entire river trail is interruption-free. For a longer marathon-pace block, select the section during planning and retain the option to pause or change the workout at a junction or obstruction. Shared cyclists and pedestrians remain part of the route.

Water, toilets and transport

City of Melbourne information documents fountains and toilets around Alexandra Gardens and Boathouse Drive. Stonnington lists drinking fountains at Como Park. These are identifiable planning points in the lower-river precinct; this guide does not infer regularly spaced refills along the full trail.

Carry supplies for the gap between the specific points you plan to use. If including a facility detour, count its distance and stops in the session. A map symbol for a fountain is a reason to plan a possible refill, not to arrive with no reserve water.

Flinders Street Station and Princes Bridge provide a CBD access combination. Choose the actual trail start after the station connection, then use the same point for the return. The relevant official maps and facility pages are linked below for planning the section rather than a single imposed start for every runner.

When to choose the Yarra

The corridor is useful when you want a longer continuous route and can plan a suitable turnaround. For marathon-pace work, the lower asphalt section may fit the intended surface better than a gravel circuit, provided the selected stretch and conditions allow it. For an easy long run, the ability to turn earlier can be more useful than completing a named trail end to end.

Choose daylight for an unfamiliar section and check access signs before proceeding. This guide makes no route-wide lighting or after-dark safety claim. Albert Park Lake offers a repeat-lap alternative if returning regularly to the same starting area matters more than exploring the river corridor.

Frequently asked questions

How long is the Main Yarra Trail?

Parks Victoria lists a 33 km trail. The distance of your run depends on the entry, exit and section chosen; the guide's CBD access point is not a claim about one measured full-course start.

What changed at Gipps Street?

Parks Victoria confirms the new ramp opened in February 2026. Use current project information rather than an older description of that interruption.

Is the Burnley half marathon exactly three 7 km laps?

Do not infer the measured half-marathon course from that arithmetic. Sri Chinmoy publishes a 7 km circuit and several event distances; consult its current course information.

How can I plan a 30 km out-and-back?

Choose a starting point with a usable 15 km outward section, then return on the same route. The distance is a training calculation, not a named surveyed turnaround.
