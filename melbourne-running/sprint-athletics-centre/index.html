Sprint Athletics Centre Running Track, Craigieburn

Free public entry from dawn to dusk, with exclusive booking exclusions. Plan around the centre’s track access and neighbouring Anzac Park facilities.

Access, fees and lighting

Hume Leisure publishes free public entry between dawn and dusk, seven days a week. Club, school and private bookings have exclusive use; public runners must cooperate with requests to move or leave. Build some flexibility into your session rather than counting on access at a particular time.

The July 2026 rate for hiring the track and pavilion is $159.95 an hour. That is an organised hire charge, separate from free casual entry. Use the operator’s booking enquiry for a group session. Aths.au lists lighting, but public access still ends at dusk.

The Craigieburn circuit

The operator lists a 400 m synthetic circuit with eight circular lanes and ten on the front straight. A 2 km lane-one block is five laps; at 5:00/km it takes 10:00. Choose a time when an exclusive booking will not interrupt the planned blocks and recoveries.

400 m pace-per-lap table

Multiply pace in seconds per kilometre by 0.4. At 4:30/km, that is 270 × 0.4 = 108 seconds, or 1:48 per 400 m.

A full outer-lane circuit is longer than 400 m. These times apply to lane one at its measurement line, or to a correctly marked 400 m start in another lane. If you move outward to give another athlete room, avoid treating that full circuit as a lane-one pace test.

Getting there and using the facilities

The entrance is at the corner of Aitken Boulevard and Marathon Boulevard, Craigieburn, in Hume. The operator lists 50 parking bays and two accessible bays, without guaranteeing a space. Hume’s Anzac Park information identifies bus stops on Aitken Boulevard and Central Park Avenue. Check your live journey and return service before leaving.

Public toilets are in the adjoining Anzac Park during public access times. Carry the water you need for the workout so a refill does not determine the recovery interval. Keep your bottle and bag clear of active lanes, and use the same start mark for each repetition.

Centre restrictions

Hume prohibits animals, motorbikes, bicycles, scooters and skateboards at Sprint. Public access covers the running track; other athletics equipment requires prior council approval. Keep those restrictions separate from what may be permitted in the surrounding Anzac Park precinct.

Frequently asked questions

Is Sprint Athletics Centre free for public running?

Hume Leisure lists casual entry as free. Exclusive club, school or private bookings can require public runners to leave. Hiring the track and pavilion is a separate paid booking.

Can I train at Sprint Athletics Centre after dark?

The operator publishes public access between dawn and dusk. A directory lists track lighting, but that does not extend casual access beyond the operator’s hours.

What is one lap at 5:00 per kilometre?

A 400 m repetition at 5:00/km takes 2:00. This assumes the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for a 400 m repetition in another lane.

Can I use the jumps or throwing equipment?

Public access covers the running track. Hume requires prior council approval for use of the other athletics equipment.
