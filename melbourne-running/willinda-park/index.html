Public use and hire hours are different

Willinda Park serves Greensborough and Melbourne’s north-east. Banyule gives the reserve address as 21A Talbot Street; the track operator also uses Beatrix Street in its location information. Use the operator’s directions for the athletics entrance when arranging the visit.

A 2023 council comparison records public track use outside bookings. The operator publishes typical hire hours of Monday–Friday, 8:30 am–4:30 pm, with other hire times negotiated. Those are hire arrangements, not a timetable promising unrestricted casual running during those hours.

What to establish before a session

For a fixed-time workout, use Willinda Park’s current operator information to establish availability. For a group, organise hire separately. The older council comparison explains why the venue is in this public-access shortlist, but current bookings determine whether it suits a particular session.

The track is listed as a 400 m polyurethane circuit with eight circular lanes and ten on the straight. Lighting is listed, without a casual-use lighting timetable in this guide. Avoid making an evening plan solely from the fact that lights are installed.

A longer interval option in the north-east

If your programme calls for 2.4 km repetitions, six lane-one laps provide that measured distance. At 5:00/km, each block takes 12:00. That format uses the circuit without needing a separately mapped road loop around Greensborough.

Allow for the whole visit when choosing an available slot: the warm-up, planned recoveries and cool-down take time beyond those twelve-minute blocks. If another booking limits the slot, shorten or reschedule the session in line with the purpose of your training day.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Toilets, parking and arrival

Banyule lists public toilets at the reserve. The published hire terms specify designated parking bays for hirers. Follow those terms and current signs when attending with a group, and leave enough time to identify the correct entry rather than assuming both published street addresses lead to the same gate.

Willinda is included for its north-eastern location and measured circuit. Compare it with the Tom Kelly track if a published availability calendar makes your session easier to arrange. The useful choice is the venue with an available window that fits the workout and travel, rather than whichever has the most lanes.

Frequently asked questions

Are Willinda's hire hours public opening hours?

The operator's Monday–Friday 8:30 am–4:30 pm window describes typical hire hours. It should not be treated as unrestricted casual access.

Where is Willinda Park?

Banyule gives 21A Talbot Street, Greensborough; the operator also lists Beatrix Street. Use current operator directions for the athletics entrance.

How long is the circuit?

The published circuit is 400 m in lane one, with eight circular lanes and ten on the straight.

What public-access evidence is available?

A 2023 council comparison records use outside bookings. Use current operator information to establish availability for a particular session.
