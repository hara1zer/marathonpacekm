Knox Park Athletics Centre Running Track, Knoxfield

A Bunjil Way track with published daily turnstile hours and a 2025 resurfacing. Events, maintenance and mowing still restrict access.

Published gate hours and the exclusions

Knox Park Athletics Centre is on Bunjil Way in Knoxfield. The council publishes turnstile access from 5 am–9 pm every day, except while scheduled athletics activities, maintenance or mowing are taking place. Those exclusions remain part of the access rule, even within the stated gate hours.

The council lists local club activities at the centre. Use its current facility information to plan around the intended date, especially if organising a longer workout or travelling for a weekend session. A published daily access window makes planning clearer, but it is not an exclusive allocation for a casual runner.

The 2025 resurfacing

Knox identifies the surface installed in the 2025 upgrade as Conispur SW and records LED lighting among the works. That current council description takes priority over older directory references to another surface product. The circuit specifications report 400 m, eight circular lanes and ten on the straight.

LED lighting is a documented facility feature. The gate window alone does not specify when the lights operate, so choose an arrival time that suits the visibility you need and follow the conditions on site.

Planning a workout on the circuit

An 800 m repetition is two lane-one laps; at 5:00/km that is 4:00. For a session built around longer 2 km efforts, each block is five laps and takes 10:00 at the same pace. Those conversions let you assess the planned work against the period available before another activity starts.

Use the marked repetition start and the current lane arrangements. If a club activity or maintenance exclusion begins, change the session timing rather than treating the 9 pm gate limit as permission to continue.

400 m pace reference

Time = pace in seconds per kilometre × 400 / 1,000. Use the lane-one measurement line or the appropriate marked start for the repetition. Full outer-lane circuits are longer.

Facilities and the journey

The community facility guide reports toilets and adjacent parking. Use Bunjil Way as the destination and the current entry signs to locate public access. Allow time to find the turnstile and facilities before starting a timed workout.

Knox Park adds a south-eastern option with a recent, council-described resurfacing and clear published gate hours. Compare it with Duncan Mackinnon Reserve if the journey or daylight availability is more convenient. Duncan’s separate perimeter path and summer exclusions create a different visit plan from Knox’s turnstile arrangement.

Frequently asked questions

What are Knox Park's public gate hours?

The council lists turnstile access from 5 am–9 pm every day, except during scheduled athletics, maintenance and mowing.

What surface was installed in 2025?

Knox describes the resurfacing as Conispur SW. This current council specification takes priority over older product references.

Do the gate hours guarantee the lights are on?

No. The council documents installed LED lighting, but the gate hours alone do not establish its operating schedule.

Where is Knox Park Athletics Centre?

It is on Bunjil Way in Knoxfield. Use current entry signs to locate the public turnstile.
