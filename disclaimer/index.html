Training & health

  • Consult a qualified professional if you have a medical condition, injury, or symptoms.
  • Adjust pacing and training to conditions (heat, hills, illness, fatigue) and your own fitness.
  • Stop if you feel unwell, dizzy, chest pain, or other concerning symptoms.

Accuracy

We aim for accuracy, but pace charts and calculators can contain errors. Always sanity-check results and race-day conditions.
