Norwegian Singles FAQ: specific answers and next steps

Use this page for practical terminology and execution questions. The overview contains the research context; the workout and plan pages contain the detailed examples.

Does singles mean one workout per week?

No. On this site it means one threshold session on a quality day rather than a morning-and-afternoon double. One weekly session is our cautious calendar example. Read the definition and its limits.

How many quality sessions belong in my week?

The name cannot decide that. Count club intervals, tempo runs, races and hard long runs before adding anything. The calendar examples keep one quality dose while adding easy-running days; they do not prescribe that dose to every reader.

What if the repetitions turn into a race?

Reduce pace, reduce the remaining work or end the quality part. Do not sprint the final repetition to make the average look consistent. Record the change and the next day’s response so that the next workout is based on what you actually tolerated.

Should a new runner start here?

A stable easy-running routine can be the more useful starting goal. The presence of three runs in a calendar does not establish readiness for threshold sessions. The suitability checklist starts from the current training problem.

Can I keep my fast long run as well?

That adds another demanding session. Compare the entire week and consider replacing the threshold day with easy running when the long run is the priority. See the two illustrative weeks.

Does the calculator find my actual threshold?

No. It applies fixed offsets to recent race pace. A 50-minute 10K with moderate experience and normal conditions produces 5:12–5:20/km. The methodology lists all offsets. The result is an estimate, not a lactate measurement.

Why is a double a different commitment?

In the identical-session comparison, one 4 × 6-minute session has 24 work minutes and two have 48. Warm-up, cooldown, travel and between-session recovery also increase the time commitment.

Is this just another name for a tempo run?

Not necessarily. Tempo can describe different efforts and structures. Compare continuous and broken 24-minute examples by their uninterrupted effort and recovery periods, not the label alone.

How do I record a treadmill version?

Record belt speed, incline and cooling conditions. At 12 km/h the setting corresponds to 5:00/km; it does not guarantee the same effort outdoors. The workout page gives the conversion example.

What if normal easy training keeps suffering?

Review the workload instead of assuming you need a more advanced version. Replace or reduce the quality session and assess the wider week. Pain, illness or unusual symptoms are not a pace-calculator problem.

Two final checks

Are the example calendars validated Norwegian protocols?

No. They illustrate scheduling choices by this site. Research and elite practice are identified separately in the overview.

What should I record after a session?

Work minutes, repetition pace, recoveries, conditions, any adjustments and the response of later easy runs. That is more informative than the training label alone.
