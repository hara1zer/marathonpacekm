An exact four-hour split reference

Illustrative calculations checked 8 October 2026 · Distance, rounding and assumptions

This page models 4:00:00 elapsed over 42.195 km: 5:41.3/km, 9:09.2/mile and 2:00:00 halfway. It is an exact reference for comparing checkpoints, rather than a plan with a sub-four buffer. Use the sub-four guide to evaluate a finish-time boundary and room for stops.

The full split table and calculator use the unrounded average. A watch set to exactly 5:41/km implies 3:59:48 across the measured distance; that approximately twelve-second difference is rounding, not a reliable race-day cushion.

Elapsed time, moving time and fixed references

A one-minute stationary stop inside a four-hour finish leaves 3:59:00 to cover the course, requiring 5:39.9/km while moving. The calculator’s elapsed checkpoints already include that stop; do not subtract it again from your recorded time.

At an official 10 km marker, compare total elapsed time with 56:53. Auto-pause can hide station or toilet time and make the watch’s moving average appear on target while the elapsed finish drifts. Keep a race clock visible for this comparison. Walking still covers distance, so it requires a different mixed-speed model.

The original four-hour reference remains useful if you edit the calculator, but printed material must match your revised goal. Use the goal-aware band button for your edited target. For custom pacing, phone cards and A4/Letter PDF output at the original four-hour target, use the custom generator.

4:00 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 4:00 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 5:41.3/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 2:00:00 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.
