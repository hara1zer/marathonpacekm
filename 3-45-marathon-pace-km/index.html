Work through a 3:45 race plan

How easily a banked minute disappears

At 3:45, the average is 5:19.9/km. Running the opening 10 km ten seconds per kilometre faster would put you 100 seconds ahead of the even plan. A slowdown of just over eight seconds per kilometre across the final 12.195 km would erase that entire gain.

This is a break-even calculation, not a claim that a particular start will cause a particular slowdown. Its value is showing how little late-race deterioration is needed to undo an early gain. The time-in-the-bank calculator lets you change those assumptions.

For a controlled opening, the pace-band generator shows the later adjustment needed to preserve 3:45. Read that adjustment before selecting it: a slower start only keeps the same finish time if later running makes up the difference. If the remaining pace no longer matches your effort, revise the goal rather than forcing a late rescue.

Check the full distance remaining after 40 km

The measured course still has 2.195 km remaining at the 40 km marker. Compare that requirement with a watch estimate based on only two kilometres.

At an even 3:45 pace, 40 km is 3:33:18. The final 2.195 km take 0:11:42. Two kilometres at the same average take 0:10:40, leaving about 62 seconds unaccounted for if you omit the extra 195 metres.

Check the goal, then plan the supplies

The pace calculation is a reference, not a fitness test. Compare a recent race using the marathon predictor, then review its assumptions against your training and the expected conditions.

3:45 planning FAQ

What pace per km is a 3:45 marathon?

The exact average over 42.195 km is 5:19.9/km. The calculator uses full precision before displaying rounded checkpoints.

What is the even halfway checkpoint?

The halfway reference is 1:52:30 elapsed, including any pauses. A rounded display is not an additional time allowance.

How many gels does this goal require?

The finish time does not determine a universal gel count. Choose a practiced hourly intake, check grams per packet, include drink carbohydrate and verify the actual scheduled average in the fueling calculator.
